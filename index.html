Why it can't wait

Salt air and humidity are eating your concrete from the inside out.

In South Florida, moisture and chlorides work their way into concrete and reach the steel reinforcement. The rebar rusts, expands, and cracks the concrete around it. That's spalling. What starts as a hairline crack or rust stain on a balcony becomes loose concrete, failed railings and an expensive structural repair.

Since Florida's condo safety reforms, milestone inspections and reserve studies are putting these conditions on paper. Associations need a contractor who can turn the engineer's report into a clear scope, a fair price and a finished job.

FAQ

Common questions

What is concrete spalling?

Spalling is when concrete cracks, flakes or breaks away, usually because the steel rebar inside has rusted and expanded. It's common in coastal South Florida buildings and gets worse over time if it isn't repaired.

Do you work with our engineer's milestone inspection report?

Yes. We build our scope directly from the engineer's report and coordinate with them through the repair and closeout process.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The on-site assessment and written proposal are free and carry no obligation.

Can residents stay in the building during repairs?

In most cases, yes. We phase the work and communicate schedules in advance so disruption is kept to a minimum.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties.
