Why it can't wait

Salt air and humidity are eating your concrete from the inside out.

In South Florida, moisture and chlorides work their way into concrete and reach the steel reinforcement. The rebar rusts, expands, and cracks the concrete around it. What starts as a hairline crack or rust stain on a balcony becomes loose concrete, failed railings and an expensive structural repair.

With building recertification requirements putting these conditions on paper, property owners need a team that can diagnose the root cause, deliver code-compliant recommendations and see the repairs through.

FAQ

Common questions

What is concrete spalling?

Spalling is when concrete cracks, flakes or breaks away, usually because the steel rebar inside has rusted and expanded. It's common in coastal South Florida buildings and gets worse over time if it isn't repaired.

Can you help with building recertification?

Yes. We work closely with licensed engineers on assessments, complete the required repairs and help you through the recertification process.
